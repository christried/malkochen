malkochen issue #330, for the Dish life spec (#318)

Dish life prototype

Three looks each for the card, the Verlauf and the Gekocht toast, built into the real app. Votes and Comments are made up per Dish; Cookings, the Board and everything else are real. Pick one per question (or a mix).

Theme Screen

1. The card

How a card shows its Votes, how many Comments it has, and that something is new in its Verlauf since you last opened it, next to the image, title, Source badge, adder, "3× gekocht" and "Neu".

Shakshuka is a New Dish with 2 unread Comments. Cremige Pilzpasta has 4 👍, 1 👎, 3 Comments, something new. Ofengemüse has 2 👍, 2 👎 and 12 Comments, all read. Gnocchi leans 👎 with one new Comment. The note icon is gone everywhere, as notes become Comments.

AQuiet row

Line icons in the bottom row after the adder's picture: the thumb most people gave, then the speech bubble with its count. Something new puts an orange dot on the bubble.

  • Calmest; the image and title stay untouched.
  • Beside "Gekocht ✓" a desktop card has about 40px, so it can't show how many voted, only which way it leans. Two-digit counts touch the button.

BGlass chip on the image

A small frosted chip down the thumb's top right, one line each for 👍, 👎 and Comments. Something new turns the chip the "Neu" orange.

  • Shows every number and fits any card width; clear of "Neu" (top left) and the Source badge (bottom left).
  • Covers a corner of the picture, and three lines are busy on a small thumb.

CIn the line under the title

Emoji with counts after "3× gekocht". Something new marks the Comment count like a highlighter.

  • Shows every number and reads like the rest of the card's facts.
  • Takes the room from the Creator, which is cut down to a letter or two on most cards.

2. The Verlauf

The Dish History at the bottom of the Dish detail: added, Cookings, Votes and Comments, oldest first, with the comment field underneath.

Same in all three: the heading becomes "Verlauf" (achievement badge, summary and "Gekocht ✓" stay); the detail's "Hinzugefügt von" line and the note are gone, since the Verlauf starts with who added it; "bearbeitet" follows the time; your own Comments have "…" for Bearbeiten and Löschen; Cookings keep the bin. Shown on Cremige Pilzpasta, and on Shakshuka (never cooked, so no Votes yet, the loop stays).

AChat

Comments as speech bubbles, yours on the right in butter. Cookings as sage pills in the middle, Votes and "added" as small centred lines. A "Neu" line marks where the unread part starts. 👍 👎 sit beside the comment field.

  • Reads like a group chat; voting and commenting in one place at the bottom.
  • Many Votes make many small lines.

BTimeline

A line down the left with one dot per entry: the person's picture for a Comment, a pot for a Cooking, the thumb for a Vote. Comments in the detail's serif. "Wie war's?" with 👍 👎 under the heading.

  • Every entry the same shape, easy to scan; Comments read like the Cooking notes today.
  • Less of a conversation; your own Comments don't stand out.

CCards like today

Each entry a row like today's Cooking History, with its date pebble. Votes next to each other fold into one row. An unread Comment gets an orange ring and "Neu". 👍 👎 as two chips with everyone's counts under the heading.

  • Closest to what the app has; the tally is visible at the top.
  • Heaviest: a box per entry gets long with many Comments.

3. The Gekocht toast

"Gekocht ✓", 👍 👎, "Kommentieren" and "Rückgängig" at 360px.

Same in all three: a thumb keeps the toast open, so you can change it (each option is shown before and after 👍); "Kommentieren" opens the Dish with the comment field in focus; the add button moves up over a toast of two rows.

AOne pill, wraps

Today's toast with the thumbs added. On a phone it breaks into two rows, "Gekocht ✓" above, the four controls below; on a desktop it's one row.

  • Least new; one row on a desktop.
  • On a phone the first row is nearly empty.

BTwo rows, "Wie war's?"

"Gekocht ✓" and "Rückgängig" on top; "Wie war's?" with 👍 👎 and a "Kommentieren" button below.

  • Asks the question out loud; the biggest targets.
  • Taller, so it covers more of the Board for its 5 seconds.

COne row, bubble button

"Kommentieren" becomes a speech-bubble button next to the thumbs, so everything fits one row at 360px.

  • Smallest; the toast keeps its shape.
  • The bubble has no word on it (its name and tooltip say "Kommentieren").

Try it yourself: branch issue-330-prototype, pnpm dev, open a Board. A dark bar at the top switches the variants (← and → cycle them), or add ?card=B&verlauf=C&toast=A to the address. The prototype is never merged.
