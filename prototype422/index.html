Questions 1 and 2

Where he comes in, and his bubble

Once the Cooking can no longer be undone (the Gekocht toast has gone), his head comes in and asks. A from the right edge, leaning in, partly behind it. B from the bottom, looking over the screen's edge. C at the toast's place, sitting on the box's corner. The bubble: A light with a comic point, B dark like the toast, C a card with a butter rim. Rows are the place, columns the bubble.

Question 3

How he comes in

slide glides in. peek looks in halfway, waits a beat with a tilt, then comes all the way. bounce hops in with a squash. Each video plays the Board opening, him asking, then "Nein": his last word with a finger on his lips, and his exit. Phone, light, bubble A, wording 2.

Question 4

Wording

Three ways he asks, with the answer buttons, his line after "Nein", and the thank-you. The screenshots use 1. In the prototype, switch with ?text=1|2|3 and the language with ?sprache=de|en|it.

Question 5

The report sheet

After "Ja", or "Snackbert melden" in the Dish History. A photo of the portion (camera or gallery), a recording (running time, stops at 2:00, record again), a few words, the line saying all of it is optional, "Senden" ("Ohne alles senden" while empty). A a list, one thing under the other. B three tiles with Snackbert holding a camera and a microphone; a tile opens its panel below. C a chat with Snackbert: what you add appears as your messages. Rows: empty, filled in, while recording.

Question 6

After "Senden"

A the sheet says thanks, with his abacus face and "Fertig". B the sheet closes, he pops up with his abacus, says one line and leaves with it. C a toast with his head. B and C also show over the Dish detail.

Question 7

"Snackbert melden" in the Dish History

Only on your own Cookings (here the newest, "Kim hat gekocht"); Mira's has none. A a pill under the line. B his head as a button beside the bin, a tick once reported. C a text link under the line. Rows: not yet reported, reported.

Try it yourself

Running it locally

Start

On branch issue-422-prototype: pnpm dev, open a Board. Everything is dev only.

The real moment

Cook a Dish ("Gekocht ✓"). Five seconds later, once "Rückgängig" has gone, he asks.

The bar at the top

Chips pick the question, ← and → switch its variant and play it again. "Frage zeigen", "Blatt öffnen" and "Bestätigung zeigen" replay. In the Dish detail the bar sits inside the panel.

What's fake

  • Nothing is sent. "Senden" only marks the Cooking as reported until reload.
  • The recording is a running clock; the photo is real but stays in the page.
  • He asks on every Cooking, also without Snackbert switched on.
