# Photos for the brag video

Stock photos of dishes for the brag video (#313): they stand in for the images of the posts a real Board is full of, on the made-up Board and in the opening grid. Each file is named after the Placeholder Image it replaces (`shared/placeholders.ts`). The video keeps about one picture in five as the drawn Placeholder, so the app's own look still shows.

All of them are **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication, <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>): free for any use, commercial too, no attribution needed. Credit is given below anyway.

The licence of each was taken from [Openverse](https://openverse.org) (checked 2026-10-18), which indexes these sources with their licences. The source pages themselves sit behind bot protection, so they could not be read from a script. Open a link below in a browser to see the licence where the photo comes from.

Resized to at most 1200 px on the long side (JPEG, quality 85), otherwise as published.
