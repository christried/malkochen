# Music for the malkochen launch video (about 20 s)

Research date: 2026-10-18. Question: where can a solo developer find and download music that may legally be used in a short promo video, published on a GitHub README, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and possibly embedded on the app's own site.

Verification legend used below:
- **[V]** read in the service's own licence, terms, FAQ or help page (fetched on the research date).
- **[P]** the primary page was found, but I could only see its text in a search snippet. Treat as likely, not confirmed.
- **[U]** could not verify from a primary source. Listed again in the open points section.

This note is not legal advice. The licence on each track's own download page governs.

---

## Recommendation

- **Best free pick: Pixabay Music, with Mixkit as the backup.** Both are free, need no attribution, allow commercial and promotional use across the platforms this video will go to, and allow editing. Neither page makes any promise about Content ID, so keep the licence page and download receipt for each track. Pixabay's own terms say attribution is not needed [V]. Mixkit's free licence covers ads and social video and forbids remixing and music-only use [V].
- **Best paid pick: Epidemic Sound, Creator plan.** Music published during an active subscription stays cleared, but only on channels that were safelisted [V]. Its Creator plan does not cover digital ads [V], so the video must not run as a paid ad. Check that the Creator plan fits a product promo before buying (open point 3).
- **Avoid:**
  - Bensound's free tier for anything promoted or posted off-line. It needs an attribution line on every video, forbids ads and paid promotion, and limits editing [V].
  - Creative Commons NC tracks (Freesound CC-BY-NC, Free Music Archive NC, ccMixter NC) for a product promo, because the video is promotional [V].
  - Free Music Archive ND tracks, because syncing them to video is a derivative work and is barred [V].
  - TikTok Commercial Music Library and Meta Sound Collection tracks in an externally made video. Both licences stop at the platform [V for TikTok, V for Meta's clause quoted in search, see open point 4].
  - Any "free" track from a random upload site without a clear licence page.

## Per-source notes

### 1. YouTube Audio Library

- Free to use in videos. The page says the library offers "royalty-free production music and sound effects to use in your videos" and that they are "copyright-safe" [V] (https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3376882).
- Attribution: Creative Commons tracks require credit "in your video's description". Tracks under the standard licence need none; filter with "Attribution not required" [V] (same URL).
- Ads and monetisation: the page says YouTube Partner Program members "can monetize videos with music and sound effects from the Audio Library". It says nothing about ads or commercial use outside YouTube [V] (same URL).
- Content ID: "Copyright-safe music and sound effects downloaded from the Audio Library won't be claimed by a rights holder" [V] (same URL). It also says YouTube "is not responsible for issues that arise from 'royalty-free' music ... from ... other music libraries" [V] (same URL).
- Editing, BPM, mood filters, stems: not confirmed on the page [U].
- Restriction here: the page is written for YouTube. Using the track on Instagram, TikTok or LinkedIn is not addressed [U].

### 2. Pixabay Music

- Free. The licence summary says "Use Content for free" [V] (https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/).
- Attribution: "You do not need to credit Pixabay or the contributor of Content when you use it" [V] (https://pixabay.com/service/terms/, "Last updated: November 18, 2024").
- Commercial use and modification: the terms grant the right "to download, use, copy, modify or adapt the Content for commercial or non-commercial purposes" [V] (https://pixabay.com/service/terms/, Section 5).
- Ads and promotion: the terms do not prohibit ads. The restrictions are on trademarks and logos, and on using Content "as part of a trade-mark, design-mark, trade-name, business name or service mark" [V] (https://pixabay.com/service/terms/).
- Content ID / claims: not addressed in the fetched text [U].
- Edit, cut, loop: modification is permitted [V] (https://pixabay.com/service/terms/, Section 5). Loop-specific wording not checked [U].
- BPM, key, mood, stems: not checked [U].
- Restriction: the pages are for general use. Keep a copy of the licence page and the download record for each track.

### 3. Free Music Archive (FMA)

- FMA does not own the works. "FMA cannot license original work to you for commercial, private, or other use" and the artist sets the terms [P] (https://freemusicarchive.org/terms_of_use, found in search; not fetched).
- Commercial use under the Creative Commons types: "You can use it for commercial purposes" for CC BY, BY-SA, BY-ND and CC0. NC types: "You cannot use it for commercial purposes" [V] (https://freemusicarchive.org/License_Guide).
- NC types also bar use "for fundraising, advertising, or promoting a product or service without further permission" [V] (https://freemusicarchive.org/License_Guide).
- ND types: "Syncing a track to video/moving images constitutes a derivative work, which is prohibited by this licence" [V] (https://freemusicarchive.org/License_Guide).
- Attribution: "You must credit the artist" for every CC licence [V] (https://freemusicarchive.org/License_Guide).
- Content ID: FMA says it cannot address Content ID claims directly, and artists should be contacted [P] (https://freemusicarchive.org/faq/, search snippet).
- Restriction: pick only CC BY or CC BY-SA tracks, and check each track's own licence page.

### 4. Uppbeat

- Free plan: each download gives a unique credit to paste into the YouTube description, valid for one video [P] (https://uppbeat.io/user-agreement, search snippet only). The primary page returned 429 when fetched [U].
- Paid plans: safelisting of channels, which removes the need for credits on each video [P] (search snippet from Uppbeat's support pages).
- Commercial and ads use, edit rules, and cancellation rules: not verified [U].

### 5. Epidemic Sound

- Creator plan: "The Creator plan is for individual creators making video content for their own channels" [V] (https://www.epidemicsound.com/our-plans/creator-plan/).
- Ads: "The Creator plan doesn't cover licensing for digital ads" [V] (same URL).
- Safelisting: "To keep your content cleared, safelist your channels by connecting them to your Epidemic Sound account before you publish" [V] (same URL).
- After cancelling: content published during an active subscription "is cleared forever, as long as your channels were correctly safelisted and you stayed within the terms of your plan." Content published after the subscription ends "is not cleared, and may be subject to copyright claims." The same applies to tracks downloaded but not used [V] (same URL).
- Business licence: "If you want to soundtrack commercial content on a larger scale, you'll need a business license" [V] (https://www.epidemicsound.com/how-it-works/license-music-legally/).
- Price: not on the fetched pages [U].
- Editing, BPM, mood filters, stems: not checked [U].
- Help-centre article on cancellation (https://help.epidemicsound.com/hc/en-us/articles/25918384209042) returned 403 when fetched. The same wording appears in its search snippet [P].

### 6. Artlist

- Claims handling: for YouTube claims where the claimant is Artlist Ltd, add the channel or video to the account's Clearlist; download the licence certificate and invoice as proof of coverage [P] (https://help.artlist.io/hc/en-us/articles/29648852943901-Understanding-copyright-and-Content-ID-claims, search snippet only; primary fetch returned 403).
- Limit: Artlist cannot clear a claim where the track was not downloaded during an active subscription, or where the use falls outside the licence [P] (same source).
- Licence terms (commercial, ads, editing, cancellation): not verified. The primary licence pages returned 403 [U].
- Price: [U].

### 7. Mixkit

- Free licence: "Items under the Mixkit Stock Music Free License can be used in your commercial and non-commercial projects for free" [V] (https://mixkit.co/license/modal/musicFree/).
- Ads and social media: the allowed list includes "Online marketing ads" and "Social Media video posts". The licence says the Music Items may be used "on any web or social media platform, including ... advertisements" [V] (same URL).
- Attribution: the page does not mention attribution [V] (same URL). Search snippet: "no attribution or sign up required" [P] (https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/).
- Remix and edits: modification is allowed, but "You're also not allowed to remix them (or incorporate in a music-only track)" [V] (same URL).
- Prohibitions: no CDs, DVDs, video games or TV or radio broadcast. No claiming them as your own, no registering them with a rights management service [V] (same URL).
- Claims: "If you receive a claim, please forward the details to team@mixkit.co for assistance" [V] (same URL).
- Terms: the User Terms were "last revised: October 2, 2025" per search snippet [P] (https://mixkit.co/terms/). Not fetched.
- Restriction: the page shows no date for the licence itself. Price: free [V].

### 8. Incompetech (Kevin MacLeod)

- Licence: the FAQ names the credit as "Title Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com) Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0" with the CC BY 4.0 URL [V] (https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/faq.html).
- YouTube monetisation: "Yes, AND you can monetize the videos. Be sure to credit me." [V] (same URL).
- Editing: "you can sing over, chop, splice, compress, lengthen, and add instruments to anything you like", and "You MUST make it clear in the credits which parts are yours, and which parts are mine" [V] (same URL).
- Content ID: the FAQ has no guidance and links to a separate article. It says ISRC numbers can help if "someone challenges your claim" [V] (same URL).
- Standard Licence (no credit): one-time $95 fee [P] (https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/licenses/ via search, not fetched).
- Version caveat: older pages still show CC BY 3.0 credit lines [P] (search snippets).
- BPM, mood, stems: not checked [U].

### 9. Bensound

- Free tier: credit required. For online videos, "you agree to include in your video description, for online videos, the attribution text and its unique license code", and the code "is valid for a single video only" [V] (https://www.bensound.com/terms-and-conditions, Section 4.2).
- Free-licence scope: free tracks cover "online videos published and accessible free of charge" [V] (https://www.bensound.com/faq).
- Ads: the Free Licence "doesn't cover advertising". Ad use appears only in the paid Professional Licence, which covers "as part of an online advertisement" [V] (https://www.bensound.com/terms-and-conditions, Section 4.2).
- Monetisation: "Yes, you can monetize your YouTube videos when using our Free License with Attribution" [V] (https://www.bensound.com/faq).
- Editing: "You can adapt the music and sound effects to fit your project by making cuts", loops and voiceovers are allowed, but "create a song, remix, or produce your own music" is not [V] (https://www.bensound.com/faq). Terms: basic edits are allowed; "make any re-mixes" is excluded (https://www.bensound.com/terms-and-conditions, Section 5.1).
- Claims: "dispute the claim on YouTube to allow our system to clear it" after downloading the track and adding the attribution text [V] (https://www.bensound.com/faq).
- All Access: certificates "are only valid for Projects published during the subscription period" and remain valid after cancelling only for projects published while subscribed [V] (https://www.bensound.com/terms-and-conditions, Sections 4.3.2 and 12.2; https://www.bensound.com/faq).
- Price: not verified. The pricing page (https://www.bensound.com/pricing) appeared in search only [U].

### 10. ccMixter

- Each track has its own CC licence. "CC BY tracks allow commercial use ... but you must give credit" [P] (search snippets from https://ccmixter.org/about and https://ccmixter.org/).
- Terms: credit "in the manner specified by the author or licensor" [V] (https://ccmixter.org/terms, Section 4).
- Advertising: "do not use the track to advertise or promote anything other than the work you create from it or yourself" [V] (same URL, Section 4). Sampling licences bar advertising [P] (search snippet).
- Responsibility: "You agree that you are solely responsible for your use of Content" [V] (same URL).
- Date: none on the terms page. Older staff posts date from 2006 to 2013 [P].
- For this video: promotion of a product is not "your own work", so the promo-advertising clause is a real risk for ccMixter tracks [V, reading of Section 4].

### 11. Freesound (sound effects only)

- Three licences: CC0, CC-BY, CC-BY-NC [V] (https://freesound.org/help/faq/).
- CC0: "you could even sell the sound, ... but you can't claim you are the author!" [V] (same URL).
- CC-BY-NC: "Commercial use is barred" [V] (same URL).
- CC-BY: credit the original creator; a sample credit line is given [V] (same URL).
- Price of an account: [U].

### 12. TikTok Commercial Music Library (CML)

- Who may use it: "made available to brands, businesses, commercial entities and advertisers (collectively, 'Business Users')". The CML is also open to individual users [V] (https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/commercial-music-library-user-terms/en).
- Where: "You may only post or share videos that include Commercial Sounds within TikTok" and "Commercial Uses outside of TikTok are not permitted" [V] (same URL).
- Business terms: when a TikTok account is used for a business purpose, the business must use the in-app Commercial Sounds page, the web CML, or "pre-clear all music rights" [V] (https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/business-terms-eea/en, clause 5(c), "Last updated: July 2025").
- Off-TikTok use needs "separate permission and licensing the necessary rights directly from the Commercial Sound rights holder(s)" [V] (same URL as first).
- For malkochen: a video made outside TikTok, which is then posted on TikTok too, is not covered by the CML [V, reading of the terms]. Posting it on TikTok from the app is covered for business accounts [V].

### 13. Meta Sound Collection

- Licence grant, as quoted in search: "Meta hereby grants you a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use the SC Audio Content for commercial or non-commercial purposes in content you create, upload, and distribute on the Meta Company Products" [P] (https://www.facebook.com/sound/collection/terms, search snippet; the fetched page returned only a title).
- Ads: the Ads Manager help page says royalty-free Sound Collection music can be added to Instagram single-image ads, in Feed, Stories and Reels [P] (https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/759279452000505, search snippet).
- Off-Meta use: "You can't use the audio separately from those products" [P] (search snippet of the terms). Blog sources also say a cut made in-app does not carry to TikTok or Shorts [P, not Meta wording].
- Business accounts: the separate licensed music library on Instagram is for personal, non-commercial use, and certain business accounts cannot access it [P] (https://help.instagram.com/402084904469945/, search snippet).
- Price: free within Meta products [P].
- The Creator Studio help page fetched returned only its title [U].

### 14. Musicbed

- Credit: "Licensee agrees to accord credit to Licensor and the Content Supplier in accordance with the instructions on the License Details" [V] (https://www.musicbed.com/license-terms, Section 2(xiii)).
- Editing: "Licensee may not materially alter, edit, combine, or lift the Licensed Content in any way" [V] (same URL, Section 2(i)).
- Reuse of posts: archival use is allowed in context, "but not to be reused or promoted in new campaigns or formats" [V] (same URL, Section 3).
- Social tiers (Individual up to 1m followers, Personal for personal channels only): search snippet only [P] (https://www.musicbed.com/knowledge-base/what-subscription-do-i-need, which redirects to https://support.musicbed.com/; the fetched support page did not include the tiers [U]).
- Price: [U].
- Date: "Last updated on 08/13/2025" [V] (same URL as Section 2).

## Open and unverified points

1. **Artlist, Uppbeat and Epidemic help article.** Primary pages returned 403 or 429. Their claims rest on search snippets and are marked [P] or [U]. Re-check before paying.
2. **Content ID on free libraries.** Pixabay and Mixkit do not promise Content ID protection. Mixkit gives a claims contact [V]. Pixabay's terms are silent [U]. Keep receipts.
3. **Epidemic Creator plan for a product promo.** The Creator plan is "for individual creators making video content for their own channels" [V]. A product promo on malkochen's own channels may or may not fit. A business licence may be needed. Ask Epidemic or check the business plan before buying.
4. **Meta Sound Collection.** The terms text is from search only (the primary terms page returned only a title). The rule that the licence covers only Meta products is [P].
5. **Price and BPM filters.** Only Bensound and Mixkit showed their filter names. Prices for Epidemic, Artlist, Musicbed and Uppbeat are [U]. Stems were not checked for any source.
6. **Free Music Archive and ccMixter.** Pages are old; the latest licence text was not confirmed beyond the fetched guide.
7. **Mixkit and Pixabay terms dates.** Pixabay terms are dated "November 18, 2024" [V]. The Mixkit date (2 October 2025) is from a search snippet [P].
8. **Legal advice.** This note reads the public terms. It does not replace checking the licence on the track's own download page.
