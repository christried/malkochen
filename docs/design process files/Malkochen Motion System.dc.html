06Clip-Hintergründe
Every exported clip (Lottie, MP4 or WebM) is rendered on a solid surface in two versions. The surface is part of the clip and matches the container it sits in, so no alpha channel is needed. Transparent video isn’t reliable on iPhone and older Android devices. Anything that has to sit over live UI is not a video: it’s drawn natively (Compose particles) or as a Lottie with a transparent background.
